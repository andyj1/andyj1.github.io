Dricks / Tipping culture

🇸🇪 Sverige / Sweden & 🇩🇰 Danmark / Denmark

Both countries include fair wages in menu prices. Tipping is appreciated but never obligatory — unlike the US. Round up or add 5–10% only for exceptional service.

☕ Caféer / Cafés

  • No tip expected. Pay at counter; say tack (thanks). Round up öre (cents) if paying cash — e.g. SEK 47 → 50.
  • Card/contactless is default everywhere (Swish in Sweden, MobilePay in Denmark).

🍽 Restauranger / Restaurants

  • Sweden: Service included. Optional 5–10% for excellent table service at nicer places (Vollmers, Bastard). Not expected at food halls, IKEA, or counter service.
  • Denmark: Similarly — service included. Round up DKK 10–50 or ~10% at fine dining. Casual lunch: zero tip is normal.
  • Check bill for serviceavgift (service charge) — rare but if present, no extra tip needed.

🍺 Barer / Bars & pubar (pubs)

  • Round up to nearest SEK/DKK 10 at craft-beer bars (Spill). Not required at self-service.

🚕 Taxi & hotel

  • Taxi: Round up 5–10%. Uber/Bolt: optional in-app tip — most locals skip it.
  • Hotel: SEK/DKK 20–50 for porter; leave nothing for daily housekeeping unless exceptional (not customary in Scandinavia).
